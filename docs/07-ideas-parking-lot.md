## 1\. Premium / Custom Digest (monetization runway)

The **weekly Action Digest stays free on every tier** — it's the habit that drives retention and upgrades. *Custom* digest capability is where we can charge later:

- **Custom cadence & recipients** — daily/weekly/monthly, per\-stakeholder delivery.
- **Role\-tailored digests** — owner gets strategy; branch manager gets today's operational actions.
- **White\-label / branded digest** — agencies send it under their own brand to clients *(natural Agency\-tier upsell)*.
- **Goal / KPI tracking** — "chasing 4.5★ — here's progress toward it."
- **Multi\-location executive rollup** — one exec summary across all branches *(Pro/Agency)*.
- **Extra delivery channels** — Slack/Teams, polished PDF export.
- **"Ask your reviews"** — a conversational AI layer over the digest data *(premium AI)*.

*Decision deferred (Omer, Aug 2026): keep digest standard; revisit premium packaging later.*

## 2\. Deferred Product & Growth Bets

- **Native mobile app** — SMB owners are phone\-first; strong Phase 2 candidate (MVP must at least be excellent on mobile web \+ push).
- **Programmatic SEO engine** — auto\-generated pages built on our own aggregate data (e.g., "best\-reviewed dentists in Kadıköy") for compounding, low\-CAC growth.
- **Integrations ecosystem** — POS / PMS / e\-commerce (Trendyol, Shopify) / Zapier / CRM for stickiness and post\-purchase request triggers.
- **Review → content generator** — turn a great review into a branded social post automatically (bridge to the engagement\-platform vision).
- **Google Q&A management** — an ignored surface on Business Profiles.
- **Deeper competitor watch / benchmarking** — powered by the aggregate data moat.

## 3\. Business & Ops — Revisit Later

- **Bootstrap vs. raise** — stay bootstrapped until the wedge is proven; revisit only if a clear reason to accelerate appears.
