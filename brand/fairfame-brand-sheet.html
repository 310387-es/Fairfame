01 — The mark

A seal, not a sticker.

The two f's of fair and fame share one crossbar — two words fused into one mark, set in a circular seal: the stamp a good name used to get. The wordmark is Geist 700 and ends in an amber full stop — the last word on a reputation.

02 — Colour

Paper, ink, amber, indigo.

The marketing surface is warm paper with ink; amber is the brand's temperature and the only decorative colour; indigo stays what it is in-product — interaction. The app keeps its own cooler tokens (docs/15); these four govern everything outside it.

04 — The murmur

Word of mouth, made audible.

The one illustration device: the town talking, faintly, in both languages. Invented phrases — never real review text — drift on paper in Lora italic; move your pointer close and the talk sharpens into ink, as if you leaned in; a click sends a quiet ring through it. Lives only outside the product (brand/murmur.js); in print it freezes into faint static text.
